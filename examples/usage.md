# Usage Examples / 使用示例

The examples below show what to send. They are not recorded outputs; your results will depend on the prompt and the material you share.

以下示例只展示「该怎么发」，不是实际输出记录；结果会随题目和你提供的素材而不同。

---

## 1. 只要分析，不要问卷 / Analysis only

**中文**

```
帮我分析这道题，不需要问卷：

USC 补充文书（选答，250 词）：
The USC Student Commitment charges Trojans to "value honest, open communication and robust debate," and to "champion ideological diversity and embrace freedom of expression." Describe a time when you disagreed with someone you know about something important to you. Did you change your mind or reach common ground?

我申请会计专业，主文书写的是一段友谊里的个人成长。
```

You can expect: a sentence-by-sentence breakdown (the quoted values, the "someone you know" and "something important to you" qualifiers, the two required follow-up questions), a word budget weighted toward the interaction and change rather than the conflict, a note on avoiding overlap with the personal statement, and 2–3 writing directions.

你会得到：逐句拆解（学校引语、「someone you know」「something important to you」两个限定词、末尾两个必答追问），偏重互动与改变而不是冲突本身的篇幅分配，与主文书避免素材重复的提醒，以及 2–3 个写作方向。

## 2. 要问卷 / Questionnaire

**English**

```
Make a questionnaire for this prompt:

Common App Prompt 5 (650 words max):
Discuss an accomplishment, event, or realization that sparked a period of personal growth and a new understanding of yourself or others.
```

You can expect: only the questionnaire content. The prompt, then six sections (for example: Identifying the Catalyst Moment → Background and Context → Response and Reflection → Evidence of Growth → Meaning and Relevance → Narrative Voice and Final Framing), each with a short Purpose and 5–7 questions.

你会得到：只有问卷内容本身。先是原题，然后是六个部分（例如：触发时刻 → 背景 → 反应与反思 → 成长的证据 → 意义与关联 → 叙事声音与收尾），每部分有简短的 Purpose 和 5–7 个问题。

## 3. 不说要不要问卷 / Not specifying

```
Describe how you plan to pursue your academic interests and why you want to explore them at USC specifically. Please feel free to address your first- and second-choice major selections. (250 words)
```

The AI will first ask whether you want a questionnaire, then continue based on your answer.

AI 会先问你要不要问卷，再根据你的回答继续。

## 4. 填完问卷后写大纲 / Outline after the questionnaire

```
这是我填好的问卷回答：[粘贴你的回答]
请按 SKILL.md 附录里的 Outline 格式帮我写一份大纲。
```

You can expect: a paragraph-by-paragraph outline (topic sentence + supporting ideas) built only from your answers, followed by a short "Strength of Outline Evaluation."

你会得到：完全基于你回答的逐段大纲（主题句 + 支撑要点），以及一段简短的 Outline 评估。
